# Independent review checklist for research manuscripts

Use this checklist for independent checking of a candidate during skill execution, before final delivery, following [the execution checkpoint](execution-check.md). It contains general review questions, not a list of known defects or expected repairs. Determine the relevant objects, claims, evidence and risks anew from the supplied paper. Do not import diagnoses, examples, locations, preferred wording or solutions from another paper or an earlier execution.

The review must respect the assigned task, the intended scholarly audience and the discipline's professional standards. Mathematical precision, faithful attribution and honest evidence are hard constraints. Apply proof-specific questions when a paper makes formal claims, and empirical questions when it relies on observations or experiments; do not require a kind of evidence that the paper's actual claims do not call for.

## G01 — Claims, assumptions and preserved scope

Identify the important claims and their incorporated setup. Determine what each claim assumes, what it concludes, and where it applies. If a previous version is supplied, compare the full contracts in both directions, including conditions or cases not used later. Distinguish an authorized change from an accidental omission, strengthening or weakening. Resolve ambiguous wording using its actual context and support; do not invent a stronger original claim from an isolated phrase. If no baseline exists, state that preservation cannot be assessed rather than inventing one.

## G02 — Definitions, terminology and usable interfaces

Check whether the objects, methods, quantities and nonstandard notation needed at a consequential use have a clear public meaning. Consider preceding setup, precise imports and accepted disciplinary conventions. Verify that definitions and methods actually supply the properties consumed later. A statement about a related or surrounding object does not automatically establish a property of the object used. Check formal statements without their proofs, with valid incorporated context retained. Do not require an extra glossary, repeated familiar definitions or one preferred notation when the meaning is already unambiguous.

## G03 — Reasoning and dependencies

Follow each important conclusion back to its support. At a nontrivial transition, identify the needed input and the argument or applicable result that supplies it. Check that changes of setting, representation or convention retain the properties used downstream. For formal work, inspect complete arguments and relevant parameter ranges; for empirical work, inspect whether the design, analysis and reported evidence support the inference actually made. Standard directly applicable results need no redundant reproof. Record an unresolved inference accurately instead of completing it privately and crediting the manuscript with that missing argument.

For an external result supporting a consequential inference, construction or applicability claim, locate what the manuscript says that result supplies, the relevant conditions and the later use. Distinguish the paper's desired conclusion from the input actually imported. State the assumed reader expertise; ordinary disciplinary knowledge may suffice, but do not assume recall of a source-specific statement. Accept clear surrounding setup, precise imports and equally usable formulations. Background attribution and familiar directly applicable results do not require an expanded account solely because they are cited. The paper need only explain the relevant input and connection; no complete theorem restatement, proof, glossary or fixed paragraph format is required.

## G04 — Sources and evidence fidelity

Independently inspect the authoritative evidence needed for consequential claims. Match the actual definitions, assumptions, versions and conclusions, including relevant alternative formulations when a comparison depends on them. Use a faithful view of notation, formulas, tables or figures when extraction could change their meaning. Separate what a source establishes from the paper's own extension or application. A missing phrase, search result or failed download does not establish absence. Source counts alone do not establish adequate coverage, and unrelated sources do not resolve a specific question.

In the execution check, save the manuscript-first reading record before consulting external sources. For the consequential inputs identified under G03, distinguish what the manuscript explicitly supplies, what ordinary professional knowledge supplies, and what remains inferred or unclear, with text locations. Then inspect the needed source passages and compare their actual conclusion, relevant conditions and role with that frozen record. Knowledge acquired from a source cannot count as an explanation already present in the manuscript. A corrected initial judgment must preserve the earlier record and explain the correction. Keep source misstatement or inapplicability, a missing explanation needed for the intended reader, and an optional clarification distinct. Require a local repair only for a demonstrated task-relevant defect; describe the needed information without prescribing a replacement sentence or display.

## G05 — Contribution and attribution

Read the contribution account with detailed support hidden. Determine what question the paper addresses, what it actually adds, and why the proposed argument or method addresses that need. Check the account against the body and the closest relevant prior work, including broader prior results that might affect the comparison. Preserve genuine limitations and imported dependencies. Do not manufacture novelty or demand a nonroutine gap where the paper's contribution is of a different legitimate kind. Supporting steps should help explain the contribution rather than obscure it in an implementation itinerary.

## G06 — Reader understanding and organization

Assess whether the intended reader can recover the main claims, the purpose of substantial constructions and the structure of the supporting argument. Locate concrete reconstruction burdens, missing connections or misleading emphasis. Consider equally clear formulations and the reader benefit of technical detail, useful notation and brief orientation. For a proposed deletion or relocation, explain what the reader gains and loses. Mere availability of a shorter version is insufficient. Do not replace professional terminology or precise statements with less accurate prose, or impose fixed lengths, paragraph templates, display counts or one proof order.

Read each affected paragraph as a whole after its additions: check whether repeated definitions, unresolved referents, indirect assertions or detached technical remarks make the reader reconstruct a connection. In statements and setup, check whether an auxiliary object or assertion still creates an avoidable detour after its name disappears or the grammatical subject changes. Locate the burden and the information that a same-strength alternative preserves; do not infer improvement from symbol counts or syntax. Retain useful computational or compatibility data, and never transfer an auxiliary object's property to the main object without justification. Apply these tests across their relevant scope, not just to wording that resembles an example.

## G07 — Final consistency and artifact integrity

Inspect the actual final manuscript after the changes interact. Check that summaries, definitions, statements, arguments, reported results, captions and conclusions agree wherever applicable. Follow changed dependencies and references, and confirm that necessary content remains reachable. When source and rendered outputs are both required, check their correspondence and the readability of relevant rendered material. Compilation, reference checks, counts and hashes establish technical integrity, not substantive correctness. Distinguish consequential presentation defects from harmless formatting warnings or optional layout preferences.

## Independent application and reporting

1. Read the supplied manuscript and any authorized baseline. From those inputs, identify the concrete questions to which G01–G07 apply; do not begin from a cached paper-specific issue list. Preserve the manuscript-first observations required by G03–G04 before opening external source content, editing criteria or editor notes. The coordinator supplies the neutral reading task first and releases this checklist and the detailed criteria afterward.
2. Check those questions using the public text and independently inspected necessary evidence. Keep the review within its assigned scope, and state missing access or evidence precisely.
3. For each applicable item, report PASS, FAIL or UNVERIFIED with locations, the relevant claim or property, and the evidence for the judgment. Explain genuine inapplicability. A general pass assertion without located work is insufficient.
4. Separate confirmed mathematical or evidential defects, uncertain interpretations and optional reader preferences. Tie any required repair to an existing task requirement or a concrete scholarly-reading problem. Count one underlying defect once even when it affects several checks. Describe the missing property without prescribing a particular sentence or formula.
5. Return the frozen findings to the executing agent for justified repairs within this execution, and recheck the affected text and dependencies before final delivery. Keep the later independent acceptance reviewer free of these findings until its own discovery is frozen. Reconcile disagreements against the actual manuscript and evidence, preserving withdrawn judgments transparently.

Paper-specific locations and conclusions belong only in the report produced by that application. They may guide this execution's own repair loop, but do not become reusable checklist entries or input hints for later fresh executions. Every new run applies these general questions afresh. This execution check supplements the assigned acceptance process and does not silently replace or expand its criteria.
