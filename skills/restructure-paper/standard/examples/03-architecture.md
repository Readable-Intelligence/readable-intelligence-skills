# Examples for 03-architecture

## Abstract examples

A theorem follows immediately from a geometric reduction and an analytic estimate, each with a long proof. State their usable conclusions and give the short deduction first; put the two full proofs in separate coherent subsections. This is logical exposition, not a requirement to reorder every short lemma.

A one-use inequality obtained by substituting into the previous bound can be an unnumbered displayed step inside its consumer, with the substitution retained. A short reusable criterion can deserve its own statement. The distinction is the mathematical work made available to the reader, not the number of lines.

If a routine verification goes to an appendix, the body must still explain the nonstandard idea that makes it applicable and state the exact result it uses. Moving all explanation with the verification fails.
