# Stage 05 — Contribution and introductory proof sketch

Rewrite the abstract as needed for accurate scope, and the introduction's methods/related-work/proof-sketch material. Read the whole paper to find the actual decisive work. Do not turn this stage into a literature survey or a summary of every proof section.

## Procedure

1. Independently inspect the closest primary predecessors and the cited versions. Determine what they already establish, in which setting and with which structures. Distinguish an unresolved mathematical question, an unproved applicability bridge, and a routine extension. Verify against both source statements and this manuscript's proofs. Do not create novelty by ignoring a predecessor's broader result.
2. Work backward from each main claim to the closest applicable prior result. Compare its actual setting, hypotheses and preserved structures with the target, then locate the manuscript's argument that supplies the missing applicability condition. A supporting implementation can be active and technically correct while leaving that condition unexplained. Select the chain: prior work reaches X; applying it here still needs Y; this paper performs Z; Z supplies Y by mechanism M. The account must identify the action across this actual gap. If the cited machinery requires a different setting and the body proves a nonroutine passage to it, do not omit that passage while describing an auxiliary construction. Cross off mechanisms already supplied by the imported results unless their present application itself requires the identified new argument. If no nonroutine gap exists, say accurately what the contribution is rather than inventing one.
3. Rank the actual new work before drafting. For each main argument, select its central obstacle and decisive mechanism. A supporting construction, compatibility check or identification does not become an independent introductory contribution merely because it is nonstandard or necessary for the proof. Give it separate space only if it solves a distinct central obstacle that cannot be explained through the principal mechanism. Write a compact contribution account around that selection, using the paragraph structure needed to make the action and its reason intelligible. Include the source context and brief orientation that help the reader follow this account. Keep necessary imported assumptions or foundational limitations explicit but separate. Do not assign a paragraph to every stage of a construction.
4. Compare a shorter account at the level of clauses as well as sentences, retaining the closest predecessor, central gap, author action, decisive reason it works, and necessary scope or attribution. Remove omitted details when the shorter account is equally clear and loses no useful reader orientation. A brief orienting clause may remain even when the core contribution would still be recognizable without it. A causally connected account of most intermediate constructions is still a proof itinerary, even when every sentence says what a step accomplishes. Do not retain successive technical identifications, transport operations or completion arguments merely by calling the whole chain an essential bridge. Refer to the body for that detailed chain, while explaining the mathematical idea and enough context to understand its role. Routine completion usually needs no mention.
5. Read the revised passage alone, with the body and notes hidden. A reader must extract what was missing, what the authors actually do and why that action works. Verify each novelty attribution again against the inspected primary sources. Keep the abstract's theorem scope faithful and its phrasing direct.

## Acceptance — all required

- The closest predecessor's actual achievement and the remaining applicability need are distinguished at the level of setting, assumptions and structures. The selected author action supplies that specific missing condition in the body. An active supporting construction that leaves the actual mismatch unresolved fails; accuracy of its local mechanism does not compensate. The gap is supported by inspected sources, not inferred from a missing phrase.
- Each contribution paragraph explicitly states this paper's mathematical action and the resolving mechanism. A paragraph describing only prior techniques, available formalism or passive possibilities fails.
- Selection has occurred among the proof's ingredients: the passage explains the central contribution rather than most of its implementation. Necessary or nonstandard auxiliary steps do not automatically earn space. The editor and independent reviewer must each attempt a shorter counterfactual account. Require revision when it exposes redundant or distracting detail without sacrificing useful orientation or clarity; a clause need not be indispensable to the contribution summary to help the reader. Classifying all clauses as “mechanism” does not establish their value. Routine completion and adaptation are omitted or collectively subordinated.
- The reader can recover the contribution from this passage alone without reading later proofs or an audit report. It is substantially shorter than an inherited technical itinerary when that itinerary contains removable material; no fixed word limit excuses dropping the mechanism.
- The actual statements, proofs, abstract and novelty claims agree. No dependence on an external result is silently converted into a result proved here.
