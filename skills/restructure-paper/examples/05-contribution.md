# Examples for 05-contribution

## Abstract example

Suppose a compact-domain theorem is used for an unbounded-domain problem. Building a familiar finite approximation does not explain why the theorem applies if mass can escape to infinity. If the paper's new uniform tightness argument removes that obstacle, the introduction must explain that action and why it works. An active paragraph about the approximation can be accurate yet select the wrong mechanism. Conversely, if tightness is automatic under the stated assumptions, do not manufacture it as a new gap. Locate the actual remaining argument before writing.

## A connected itinerary can still fail

“First we build a coupling preserving the error. We transport it to a truncated model, identify its boundary values, compare two completions, and pass to a limit while retaining the normalization” can be accurate and causally connected. It still spends the introduction on the proof route. If the gap is loss of uniformity across dependent blocks, and the coupling is the decisive new idea, explain how that coupling controls the error. The other operations belong in the body unless one resolves a separate central obstruction. Merely replacing “first/then” with “because/therefore” does not pass the selection test.
