# Contract shared by every editing stage

Edit the supplied manuscript for researchers in its field who have not read its construction before. Deliver an actual complete revised source, not a recommendation or compliance report. Read the whole manuscript before deciding local scope. Use the mathematical claims, definitions and complete arguments as the authority; do not treat the author's wording, ordering or existing names as authoritative editorial choices.

Preserve hypotheses, quantifiers, objects, mathematical types, operations, equivalence levels, coefficients, units, actions, signs, parameter dependence, and all complete arguments. This permits changing theorem wrappers, names, notation, order, paragraphing and proof placement. Removing an unnecessary name is different from removing the structure it denotes. Preserving an argument is different from preserving a standalone lemma. Never invent a stronger theorem to simplify a statement. If an actual mathematical gap needs new research, identify it precisely and leave that condition unverified; an editorial explanation cannot certify it away.

Use conventional professional terms supported by definitions in primary sources. Do not introduce a label merely because it makes an editorial plan convenient. Ordinary descriptive sentences and local indices do not require coined technical names. Explain mathematical mechanisms in the paper; keep inventories, source audits, DAGs and acceptance records outside it.

After adding a definition, convention, citation or explanation, reread the whole affected paragraph with its neighbors. Check repeated definitions, indirect assertions, unclear referents and technical remarks whose connection to the argument is unstated. Integrate or replace overlapping sentences, or relocate material to its point of need within the assigned stage's scope. Preserve every necessary qualification and keep required setup available before use. Accurate additions do not by themselves make coherent prose. A phrase made of established terms still fails if the local context does not identify its objects, operations and relationships; unpack the assertion instead of merely renaming it.

Apply the assigned stage across its complete scope. Fix all instances of a defect class, including unnumbered constructions. Do not stop after finding one illustration. Make substantive changes where needed, while retaining passages that already satisfy the tests. An unchanged passage is acceptable only after its actual reader-facing function has been checked.

Learn the reasoning in the included examples; apply the same question to this manuscript without copying example vocabulary or mathematical conclusions. They illustrate failure patterns, not replacement templates or exhaustive lists. Check other manifestations across the relevant text. Keep these abstract teaching examples in the skill; do not add them to the manuscript merely to demonstrate compliance. No example establishes a fact about the manuscript.

In independent work, read only the supplied manuscript, this assembled prompt, your own outputs and primary literature you independently retrieve. Do not inspect prior rewrites, other agents' outputs, old acceptance records, or surrounding workspace files. Tools may open source URLs and fetch raw literature. Existing judgments are not evidence, even when they say independently reviewed or verified.

Write `main.tex` (or the manuscript's source format) and `changes.md` in the assigned output directory. The latter records original and new passage locations, reasons, source versions and exact locators for source-dependent edits, and unresolved conditions. Record the actual literature inspected; a search result is not a read source. Keep the note proportional to actual decisions. Do not use its explanation to compensate for missing manuscript prose.

Compile when the environment supports the supplied format; repair harmless build problems and report the exact changes. Preserve labels when possible and update every affected reference. Check the mathematical meaning of edits and their downstream uses. Counts, hashes, compilation and a self-awarded PASS do not establish substantive acceptance.

Completion also requires that the affected paragraphs and statement/setup passages work as connected prose. In the existing changes record, support a required repair with a located reading burden and explain how the revision removes it without losing information. Examples include a repeated definition that forces readers to check whether its meaning changed, a referent they must reconstruct, or an intermediate assertion they must undo to recover the result. A shorter sentence, fewer symbols or a preferred grammatical form alone is not evidence of improvement. Equally clear alternatives are stylistic preferences. Apply this check within the existing pass and review, not as another round.
