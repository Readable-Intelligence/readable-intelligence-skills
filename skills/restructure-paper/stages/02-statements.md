# Stage 02 — Formal statements

Rewrite every theorem, proposition, lemma, corollary and immediately necessary setup. Read their proofs and all uses to determine the exact claim. Preserve the mathematics; do not preserve the old sentence form or introduced symbols by default. Make corresponding downstream wording/reference repairs, without reorganizing entire proof branches in this stage.

## Procedure

1. Recover each result's logical contract from the whole original: its subject, fixed auxiliary data, assumptions, usable conclusion and consumers, including definitions and standing conventions explicitly incorporated by reference. Locate the original passages binding each object, additional structure and identification. Unpack each assumed structured object into the components and maps actually needed by the result: its underlying objects, any base or projection, actions and specified identifications. For each indispensable relationship, record the exact revised statement or preceding clause expressing that relationship. An identification of the outer object, even with some extra structure preserved, does not by itself identify its chosen base, projection or other nested component. Test those bindings separately against the original contract. Giving a datum’s provenance, existence or coefficient field does not supply them. Recover the full ambient scope from that setup before enumerating an implicit parameter or choosing a setting: cases not used by a later consumer still belong to the original result. Check both original-to-revision and revision-to-original scope, not only applicability at the consumers. Do this before asking whether the revised statement is readable in isolation from its proof. An existing contextual binding may need to be made explicit in preceding setup; this clarification is not a new assumption. Do not replace the original contract by a weaker isolated-sentence reading and then report the resulting missing binding as a mathematical gap. Conversely, a proof's need alone supplies no hypothesis: an underlying-object isomorphism does not identify additional structures unless the original actually incorporates that identification. If the original supports conflicting contracts, record the conflict without guessing an additional theorem. Preserve a property of the specified structured object as such, rather than existence of some other structure. Put indispensable assumed bindings in the statement or reachable preceding setup; a forward reference may explain them but must not be their sole readable location in the revision. The derivation of a model or identification belongs in the proof; its presence or naming in the statement is decided by the output role and clarity comparison in steps 2–3. Check each invocation against this recovered contract; do not silently strengthen or weaken it.
2. Inventory the objects, quantities and auxiliary assertions in every result and its preceding setup, including unnamed intermediates, unchanged abbreviations and normalization definitions. Determine what the result communicates before choosing its grammatical subject. Removing a symbol, replacing it by a description, or making the main object the subject can leave the same unnecessary intermediate claim. Classify each by its actual role: input parameter needed to express hypotheses; substantive output or compatibility data; existential witness used to express the conclusion; or proof construction. Naming an input family can be useful when its components, maps or simultaneous conditions must be specified precisely. Such a name need not reappear in the conclusion or a later result. Compare an existential-witness formulation with the direct assertion at the same strength. Keep a conventional witness or equivalence display when it communicates the conclusion as clearly; remove the name when it adds avoidable declaration or memory burden. Mere availability of a paraphrase is not a reason to rewrite. Move names for proof-only constructions into the proof. Distinguish bare existence of a model from an explicit computational realization: a concrete complex, geometric model, formula or comparison whose specified data lets readers compute or apply the result can be substantive output. Its symbols may remain when they make that content clearer, including when its consumer is another proof. Replacing them by a long verbal encoding is not automatically an improvement. For a precursor related to the actual subject by a fixed conversion, use the complete-formulation comparison in step 3; being defined or reused does not by itself make that precursor substantive output. Repeating a disposable name later does not rescue it. Apply the same test to preceding setup: hide the proof, remove each construction block in turn, and ask which definitions or hypotheses the result and its actual consumers still need. If a named precursor contributes only to establishing this result, rather than specifying its assumptions or substantive output, move its definition and formula into the proof. Its use in this result’s own proof alone is not a reason to retain it in preceding setup. A short plain-language explanation of the mathematical subject may remain, but it must not reintroduce the removable construction or its notation as “motivation”.
3. Compare complete alternatives at the same mathematical strength, considering the statement together with its necessary setup. The test concerns what the reader must reconstruct, not whether a sentence begins with the main object or uses an existence verb. Identify the usable assertion and distinguish it from the construction that proves it. For each auxiliary object, quantity or assertion, decide whether it carries essential assumptions, independently useful output, a specified choice or compatibility, a shared convention, or only proof work. Keep essential data in the statement or necessary preceding setup; place reusable conventions where their scope is clear and proof-only material in the proof. Moving the same avoidable construction into a prefatory sentence does not reduce the burden.

   A property of an auxiliary object does not automatically become a property of the main object: preserve the exact relation, chosen structures and equivalence level. An existence or model formulation can be the right conclusion, but it earns retention through its actual information and reader benefit, not its grammatical form. Test each candidate separately; a useful model does not justify every precursor surrounding it. If a fixed conversion connects an auxiliary target to the intended result, compare the complete formula with the conversion performed against the auxiliary formulation, retaining all parameters and restrictions. Prefer neither by default. Record the complete alternative and individual retention or relocation reason for every output-witness or precursor candidate, including unchanged abbreviations, in the existing changes record. Identify the concrete burden removed or the computational, conceptual or compatibility benefit retained. Fewer symbols, a changed subject, repetition elsewhere, or an inventory declaration cannot settle this judgment. Check other manifestations, including unnamed models, nested existence clauses, indirect computed quantities and front-loaded setup; the examples are not an exhaustive pattern list.
4. Apply economy to assertions as well as names. Move an assertion serving only as a proof mechanism into the proof, preserving its full mathematical content there; removing its notation or renaming it as an intrinsic action does not remove the assertion. Keep a mechanism as formal output when its specified data or separate use makes it substantive under steps 2–3. Assess repeated equivalences and additional consequences by what they let the reader infer or apply. Remove redundant assertions, or place explanatory consequences nearby when they clarify the conclusion; retain separately useful output. No particular sentence form or shorter paraphrase establishes redundancy. Do not delete a hypothesis, important dependence, noncanonicity or unit/action qualification just because it is short enough to overlook. When extracting or checking a definition, verify its actual mathematical type from its construction, not merely that a domain and codomain have been written. Carry its well-definedness restrictions with it. Separately check the domain of every identity or equivalence asserted about it: an operation can exist on a larger class than the class where that law holds. Compare the quantified objects with the hypotheses of the cited or preceding argument, including boundary and sign cases; a displayed bound need not imply the original domain restriction or “sufficiently large” qualification.
5. Check every result as a reader would encounter it: from its statement and preceding explicit setup, without reading its proof. Then check each actual invocation against that statement. Traverse each complete proof from beginning to end, including unchanged formulas, and trace the first uses of its statement notation in the rest of the manuscript, including introductory formulas. For every display in this traversal, identify the local objects, maps and actions not bound inside it, and resolve each to an actual declaration or explicit notation import. At a first nontrivial use, the necessary meaning must be in preceding setup or an immediately attached defining clause; an unreferenced definition that appears later does not supply a declaration there. Do not restrict this scan to theorem inputs/outputs or symbols affected by editing. Record the display label or source location, each nonstandard local symbol's full parameter signature, domain and normalization, and its declaration location. A one-parameter object does not declare a family with extra parameters; a formula under another name does not declare an alias; a definition on one space does not by itself define the same symbol on a different quotient. Unambiguous established notation may use field conventions and ordinary bound variables need no artificial entries. A result-level “checked” row cannot replace this display-level traversal. Repair missing or incorrectly typed bindings in the manuscript. A declaration entry stays unresolved until its construction and each claimed law have been checked on the stated domain. When a proof invokes an assumed compatibility or component identification, link that use to the exact preceding clause recorded in step 1; the enclosing structure’s name or a generic citation alone does not locate that binding. Only print the components actually needed to check this result; do not repeat an entire standard definition or move identifications established by the proof into its assumptions. Use this same traversal to close the name decisions against the final source. Link every named definition in a statement or its necessary setup to its role decision in steps 2–3, including names newly introduced during declaration repair. For a fixed conversion, check each intermediate named quantity separately. A successful declaration entry does not establish a reason to retain that name; an omitted precursor decision remains open. Include the concise declaration inventory, these links and display coverage in changes.md; do not create another inventory or terminology survey. Mathematical meaning must agree in both directions between original and replacement.

## Acceptance — all required

- Each result states a usable conclusion about its subject with the exact assumptions and structure/equivalence level.
- Every auxiliary object or assertion, named or unnamed, passes the role-sensitive same-strength comparison. Removing its symbol or changing the grammatical subject does not pass if the reader still has to reconstruct an avoidable intermediate claim. Required relocation or deletion is supported by the located burden and a complete alternative preserving all useful data; witness/model language is neither prescribed nor prohibited. A conventional existential witness may remain when it expresses the result as clearly as the direct alternative. Mere repetition, use solely in constructing the proof, or comparison with an incorrectly strengthened direct alternative does not justify retaining a name that adds avoidable burden. Explicit computational output may use notation when a verbal replacement would obscure the same data. Useful input notation that makes several hypotheses or compatibility conditions precise is permitted; its absence from the conclusion or later statements is not a failure.
- No hypothesis or output needed downstream is hidden only in the proof. The result and its reachable preceding setup suffice to check its uses.
- Redundant commentary, proof construction instructions and unnecessary conclusion bundles have been removed where they create a demonstrated reading burden. The revised statement and surrounding setup read coherently after additions or relocations, with no confusing duplicate definition, unclear referent or disconnected qualification. Necessary distinctions remain explicit; a property of an auxiliary object has not been silently transferred to the main object. Equally clear arrangements remain stylistic choices.
- Every changed result is mapped to its proof and consumers, with the same bound objects, specified structures, identifications and full original ambient scope, including cases not used later; no fixed datum has become an existentially available alternative. Locally introduced notation throughout the statements, their complete proofs and actual uses is declared, whether inherited or moved; no internal proof display is exempt. There is no strengthened claim, silently weakened qualification or lost complete argument.
