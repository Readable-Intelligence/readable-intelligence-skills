# Part 04 — Local proof exposition

Revise the proof text throughout the manuscript, including unnamed constructions and appendices, so that its nonstandard steps are understandable from the page. Preserve its mathematical claims and complete arguments. Do not replace the global architecture or introductory contribution discussion in this part.

## Procedure

1. Read every proof and unnumbered argument and first identify its actual transitions: new constructions, changes of category, base or convention, limits, parameter choices, coherence and applications of imported results. For each nonstandard transition locate the manuscript relation that connects its input to its output. Do this before ranking passages for expansion. A short inherited assertion that constructions are unchanged, compatible or standard is a candidate to check, not evidence that the check is unnecessary. Distinguish a directly applicable standard identity from an application that must transport additional objects or structure. Then prioritize the missing mechanisms with the greatest burden of reconstruction; record why each needs explanation here. Do not limit coverage to named main lemmas or already long proofs.
2. For each passage work backward from every operation and compatibility actually consumed downstream. Give each nonstandard obligation its own entry in changes.md: the input actually established, with its mathematical structures and hypotheses; all parameters; the target structures and relation; imported or internal input location; and manuscript location of the connecting mechanism. For an imported construction, record the source's structures before translating them into the manuscript's convention. An identification of underlying objects may fail to preserve an operation needed here. If the source changes that operation or expressly limits preservation, the entry must expose the missing transport, rather than describe the source as already providing the desired structure. Do not close a structured construction with one grouped “compatibility checked” verdict. A calculation for one operation does not check another, and separate operation formulas do not establish a compatibility between them. For each change of convention, evaluate the conversion on a general input and compute the required relation, including parameter interactions. A formula after fixing or dropping a parameter does not check the full construction. An entry may close with a directly applicable standard identity or imported theorem and its precise applicability; otherwise put the missing relation in the paper. Separate a missing explanation of a valid inference from a missing mathematical argument.
3. Put the necessary reconstruction into the manuscript at its point of use. Introduce a complicated definition by the problem it solves. Explain indices by their mathematical roles. Begin a substantial multi-step proof with its operative idea, rather than “we verify the claims.” Divide it at changes of mathematical task using short descriptive transitions or headings where useful. A title plus the old dense paragraph does not constitute a repair.
4. Expand the difficult inference rather than routine algebra around it. For each nonstandard compatibility, transport, descent, correction or duality step, compare its actual established inputs with the target in the manuscript. If the step invokes an imported result, independently read the exact source, its hypotheses and surrounding limitations. Compare each structure consumed here with the structure it actually supplies before deciding that the result is directly applicable. Naming a normalization or convention does not establish the transport of an operation. Before claiming a difference in mathematical strength, type, sign or variance, both editor and reviewer must inspect the decisive formula in the rendered original or reliable exact-version mathematical source, including decorations and surrounding hypotheses. Plain PDF extraction is insufficient for such a discrepancy; record the faithful reading, or leave it unverified if no faithful view is available. If the inputs were proved internally, locate those arguments; do not manufacture an external-source obligation. Write the bridge where the category, base, parameter domain, variance or normalization changes. In a change of base or parameter, identify the fixed data or invariant that preserves the required property; a property on one fiber does not automatically hold in another. In a change of convention, display the general correction and show how it enters the needed identity; checking only a unit, zero or other distinguished case does not verify the family law. Saying “the normalization is included,” “the same data are used,” or “the criterion forces it” cannot replace that argument. This includes preliminary geometric input and other unnumbered prose. Give the intermediate relation or invariant the reader would otherwise have to invent. A directly applicable imported theorem need not be reproved, and routine algebra need not be expanded equally.
5. Reread with the private reconstruction hidden. The paper must explain why the argument works, not merely contain enough clues for an expert to discover it. If reconstruction needs a new unproved mathematical input, do not camouflage it with extra prose: identify the exact unresolved obligation.

## Acceptance — all required

- Every proof and unnumbered construction was considered; every nonstandard transition has an explicit manuscript repair or a located demonstration that the existing prose already supplies the mechanism. Selection for expansion cannot exempt an unchecked compatibility assertion. Directly applicable standard identities need no redundant proof.
- A reader can say why the construction/lemma is being introduced, which step resolves the obstruction and what is established afterward at the relevant point in the text.
- Missing indices, intermediate maps, parameter dependencies and structure-preservation obligations are explained where needed. No substantive inference is supplied only in `changes.md` or by the reviewer.
- The proof's parts follow mathematical tasks without gratuitous fragmentation. Short standard deductions remain short; long proofs do not start with unexplained machinery or a list of procedural promises.
- Every nonstandard cited reduction exposes the actual imported conclusion and the necessary bridge to the manuscript's target, including the invariant or general correction needed when the setting or convention changes; the editor and reviewer have checked that the citation really supplies the claimed input. A source pointer alone is not the bridge.
- Every added explanation follows from stated inputs or verified primary-source hypotheses. No new theorem, stronger equivalence or unproved simplification has slipped in.
