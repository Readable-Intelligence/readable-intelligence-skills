# Part 06 — Complete-paper repair

Independently read the entire supplied manuscript. Apply all five part specifications included below in this assembled prompt, repairing every remaining failure in the paper itself. If this is a production run, the supplied manuscript is the current draft; do not trust any prior agent's assertions that a part passed. If it is an untouched draft, the same criteria apply from scratch. You are an editor, not your own final acceptance authority.

## Composition of part scopes

Execute procedures 01–05 as separate passes on the evolving manuscript, in that order. A part-specific scope restriction governs only that pass: for example, pass 01 does not reorganize the paper, while pass 03 must repair its architecture. These restrictions are not global bans in the complete-paper assignment. Likewise, preserving the introduction during pass 04 does not prevent its required revision during pass 05. Produce one manuscript, with all five acceptance sets satisfied together. Revisit an earlier pass when a later change affects its conditions. The final independent-review obligation belongs to the coordinator and separate reviewer; the editor supplies actual evidence and does not certify itself.

## Procedure

1. Make a fresh pass over all four priorities: source-supported terminology/notation; useful, precise statements; the introduction's proof sketch; global and local proof understanding. Independently retrieve and open the necessary raw literature; do not consult previous source audits or editor notes. Prior verdicts, checklists and edit rationales are not evidence.
2. Apply the earlier parts' procedures to actual defects. Produce a complete manuscript with substantive repairs, not a list of issues. Compare final statements and their incorporated setup directly with the supplied manuscript, preserving every original case and structural property, including those unused by later results. Reconcile later edits against the final public text, not earlier pass records. At each inference, check that the actual definition or applicable result supplies the structures and stability under operations it needs; an ambient convention alone does not establish these for a selected collection. Trace every nonstandard local expression and alias to the public declaration or explicit import supplying its full meaning at that use; a similar name or a private explanation is insufficient. Keep necessary mechanisms and complete proofs reachable.
3. Recheck the introduction without the body, formal statements without their proofs, and the main proof's assembly without detailed supporting proofs. In the statement view, apply part 02's role-sensitive same-strength test to retained names and construction blocks, including their preceding setup, against the final statement and actual consumers. Keep equally clear conventional formulations; relocate proof-only machinery when it adds avoidable setup or memory burden, while retaining useful proof shorthand in its proof. Then read each difficult local argument without your notes. These views must all succeed; a successful full reconstruction by an expert does not excuse failure of the appropriate reading view.
4. Check every mathematical obligation in the supplied input and every altered dependency against the final text. The coordinator and final reviewer separately compare with the preserved author original for cumulative preservation. Compile, inspect relevant rendered pages, resolve references and confirm bibliography versions. Distinguish a technical build success from substantive acceptance.

## Acceptance — all required

- All acceptance conditions in parts 01–05 hold simultaneously in the actual manuscript, with complete scope and necessary primary-source evidence.
- The separate reading views recover how the main results are proved, exact usable statements, main-proof composition and local mechanisms from manuscript prose, without private explanations.
- No earlier improvement has been undone by later shortening, reorganization or terminology replacement. No dormant original defect is excused because it predates the rewrite.
- Full mathematical preservation and output integrity have been checked. An unresolved inference required within the manuscript, an unsupported application, or inaccessible necessary source evidence prevents passing its affected condition. A clearly identified imported result is checked for its stated conclusion and applicability; using it does not require independently reproving its foundations. Preserve and disclose that dependence without claiming those foundations were verified.
- The final independent reviewer, a different agent, receives the actual output, its input and the preserved author original rather than your assertion that these requirements passed.
